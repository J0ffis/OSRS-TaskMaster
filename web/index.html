OSRS TaskMaster

What should you grind next?

Get suggestions drawn from your levels, quests, diaries, combat achievements, collection log, pets and goals. Your data comes from the OSRS TaskMaster RuneLite plugin, so no outside services are asked for anything. Your bank and wealth are never read.

  1. Start RuneLite with the plugin. Double-click Start TaskMaster.bat and log in.
  2. Copy your profile. Open the OSRS TaskMaster panel on RuneLite's sidebar and click Copy my profile.
  3. Paste it here. Your profile stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

  2. Run the plugin. Paste the token into RuneLite → OSRS TaskMaster → Sync token, then log in.
  3. Fill in the gaps. Open the collection log in-game and click Search once.
