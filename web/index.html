OSRS TaskMaster

What should you grind next?

Enter your name to get three suggestions drawn from your levels, quests, diaries, combat achievements, collection log, pets and goals. Your data comes from the OSRS TaskMaster RuneLite plugin on this PC, so no outside services are asked for anything. Your bank and wealth are never read.

  1. Run the server. .\start-server.ps1 prints a sync token.
  2. Run the plugin. Paste the token into RuneLite → OSRS TaskMaster → Sync token, then log in.
  3. Fill in the gaps. Open the collection log in-game and click Search once.
